# Cake Atelier — Luxury Artisanal Cake Shop

An award-winning, cinematic website for **Cake Atelier**, an artisanal cake shop specializing in handcrafted luxury cakes, bespoke celebration tiers, and fine patisserie.

Designed with editorial typography, restrained color harmony, purposeful motion, and bespoke interactive features.

---

## 🎨 Creative & Aesthetic Direction

- **Art Direction:** Inspired by high-end French patisseries and contemporary editorial design.
- **Color Palette:**
  - `Warm Ivory (#F5F0E8)` — Primary vellum canvas
  - `Deep Chocolate (#2E1D18)` — High-contrast typography & deep accents
  - `Muted Caramel (#B88A63)` — Refined accent for indicators and active states
  - `Soft Cream (#EBE2D5)` — Tactile surfaces and dividers
  - `Burgundy (#682F3B)` — Precision validation and badges
- **Typography:**
  - Display: *Playfair Display*
  - Body & UI: *Inter*

---

## ✨ Features & Storytelling

1. **Cinematic Hero:**
   - Staggered word entrance (*"Made with love. Remembered forever."*)
   - Cursor-responsive parallax on luxury cake photography
   - Vertical animated scroll indicator

2. **The Art of Baking:**
   - Editorial split layout with craft statistics (*"Not Just Baked. Carefully Created."*)
   - Clip-path reveal on scroll

3. **Signature Creations Gallery:**
   - Interactive category filtering (*All*, *Chocolate & Ganache*, *Botanical & Fruity*, *Caramel & Praline*)
   - Hover magnification and detail overlay
   - Modal detail experience with servings, layers, and flavor breakdown

4. **The Making Process:**
   - Chronological 4-stage timeline: *01 Select*, *02 Prepare*, *03 Decorate*, *04 Deliver*
   - Staggered step animations

5. **Interactive Atelier Configurator (Bespoke Studio):**
   - Live selector for occasions, tiers, flavor profiles, and finishes
   - Real-time price and lead time calculation
   - One-click transfer to pre-populate custom inquiry modal

6. **Testimonials & Final CTA:**
   - Client stories and quotes
   - Full-bleed atmospheric CTA section

7. **Motion & Interaction Polish:**
   - Custom fluid cursor with dynamic hover states (`VIEW`, expanded target)
   - Magnetic button physics with inertia and snap-back
   - Instant form validation with loading feedback and confirmation summary
   - Full `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` accessibility

## 🚀 Local Development

To run the project locally:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/lohithalanki-hue/cake-atelier.git
cd cake-atelier

# Serve locally
npx serve . -l 3456
```

Open [http://localhost:3456](http://localhost:3456) in your browser.
