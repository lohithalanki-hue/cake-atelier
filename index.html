Our Philosophy

Not Just Baked. Carefully Created.

Every cake begins with an idea and a conversation. We take the time to understand what makes your celebration unique, then craft something that captures that feeling in flavour, texture, and form.

We source our ingredients from producers we trust — Belgian chocolate, Madagascan vanilla, seasonal fruits from local farms — because the quality of what goes in determines the quality of what comes out.

The Architecture of Flavor

01 / The Cocoa Terroir

Single-Origin 72% Valrhona

Harvested from organic shade-grown groves in the Ecuadorian basin. Conched for 72 hours to yield deep notes of dried black mission fig, toasted mahogany, and warm brioche.

Direct-Trade Cocoa 48h Cold Ferment
02 / The Silk Core

Tahitian Bourbon Vanilla Cream

Whole fragrant vanilla pods hand-scraped into raw sweet cream from Isigny Sainte-Mère. Whipped cold into a cloud-like mousse that melts at precise body temperature.

Tahitian Pods Normandy Crème
03 / The Architectural Crown

24K Edible Gold & Chocolate Sails

Tempered dark chocolate sails poured over frozen Carrera marble, hand-leafed with Florentine 24-karat gold leaf and crystallized botanical pansy petals.

24K Gold Leaf Pressed Botanicals

Texture Profile

Ternary Genoise Crumb

Zero synthetic emulsifiers. We rely on the natural tension of organic pasture-raised egg foam, creating a delicate crumb matrix that drinks in espresso liqueur without softening.

Pasture Eggs Espresso Reduction
Aromatic Balance

IGP Piedmont Hazelnut Praliné

Caramelized in copper basins with unrefined cane sugar, then stone-milled into a satin paste with hand-flaked Maldon sea salt for a sharp, addictive counterpoint to rich cocoa.

IGP Hazelnut Maldon Flakes
